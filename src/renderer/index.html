Getting started

  1. Stand 1–3 m from the camera and show your hand: control starts by itself (the status turns green).
  2. Move your hand: the hand pointer on screen is your cursor.
  3. Not accurate? Calibrate the movement area (below).
  4. To pause: hold an open hand still. To stop everything: the red button or the keyboard shortcut.

Gestures

🖐️ Your handMoves the cursor (the centre of the palm steers it). The hand pointer on screen is the cursor
🤏 Pinch thumb + indexLeft click. Held and moved = drag; two pinches = double click
🤏 Pinch thumb + middleRight click
✌️ Index + middle upScroll: move your hand up/down
🤏 Thumb + ring finger, heldAction 1 (default: the launcher with big tiles). Keep your index finger up
🤏 Thumb + little finger, heldAction 2 (default: task view). Both are configurable in Settings → Actions
🙌 Two handsOptional (Settings → advanced): one hand steers the cursor, the other clicks, drags and scrolls
✊ Fist, held stillAction 3 (default: minimise the window under the cursor)
🖐️ Open hand, held stillPause / resume (the bar fills up). Control also starts by itself when your hand is first seen
⌨️ Ctrl+Alt+KEMERGENCY LOCK from the keyboard (again = unlock, into pause)

Tip: rest your elbow and move your forearm and wrist. To click, don't hesitate: pinch and release decisively.
