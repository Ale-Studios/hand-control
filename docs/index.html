HAND CONTROL

Control your PC with your hands.

Move the cursor, click, scroll and move windows with simple gestures in front of any webcam — even from a few metres away. Free, open source, and everything stays on your PC.

What it does

Your hand is the cursor

A round dot follows the centre of your palm, smoothed so it stays steady. It is drawn above every window.

Pinch to click

Thumb and index together = click; two quick pinches = double click; thumb and middle = right click.

Fist to move windows

Close your hand on any window and move it: the whole window follows. Open the hand to drop it.

From 1 to 3 metres

Calibrate the movement area once, then control the PC from the sofa or a standing desk.

Private by design

Recognition runs on your PC (MediaPipe, on the GPU). No account, no cloud, no recordings; network requests are blocked.

Safe

An emergency lock on the keyboard, an open hand to pause, and the physical mouse always wins.

The gestures

Open hand, held stillActivate or pause (a bar fills up). At start, control turns on by itself when your hand is seen.
Move your handMoves the round dot: that is your cursor.
Pinch thumb + indexClick · two quick pinches = double click · held and moved = drag files or select text.
Fist + moveMoves the whole window under the cursor, from anywhere on it.
Pinch thumb + middleRight click.
Index + middle upScroll: move the hand up or down.
Thumb + little finger, heldMinimise the window under the cursor (configurable).
Thumb + ring finger, heldA launcher with big tiles: files, search, keyboard, zoom… (configurable).

Privacy

  • The camera image is analysed in memory and immediately forgotten: never saved, never sent.
  • The recognition engine and the model are files inside the app: nothing is downloaded at run time.
  • Every internet request from the app is blocked, and the app counts them for you (it should always say 0).

Safety

  • Ctrl+Alt+K locks everything at once (if another app owns it: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+K). Only the keyboard or the button unlocks — never a gesture.
  • Touch the physical mouse and hand control steps aside for a moment.
  • Losing your hand for a while pauses control; a test mode shows what each gesture would do without touching anything.
